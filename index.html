Arrow Dungeon Privacy Policy

Updated 12 November 2026 · applies to the Arrow Dungeon app for Android (package com.elephantlogix.arrowdungeon)

English中文

Arrow Dungeon has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play, and ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress, stars, coins, items, settings and language choice in the app's own storage on your device. From version 0.13.3, it also stores your selected age range locally to apply advertising protections; it does not ask for your name or exact date of birth. We do not receive this saved game information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the local copy. Android backup, if enabled and available, may restore some app data under your Google backup settings.

Purchases

Coin packs, the starter kit and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app uses purchase tokens on your device to grant purchases and confirm them with Google Play. We do not operate a purchase server. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

From version 0.13.3, new coin-pack purchases provide coins only. The starter kit and Remove ads permanently remove between-floor ads; that benefit can be restored using the same Google Play account. Reward videos remain optional. The update preserves earlier buyers' ad-free benefit recorded in their existing save. Consumed coin packs cannot be restored through Google Play, so that older benefit depends on retaining or restoring the save. Contact us if you lose it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: short reward videos that you choose to watch (for extra undos, hints or a revive), and, after the first area, an occasional full-screen ad between floors. Ads never appear during a floor. The purchase benefits above explain when between-floor ads are removed. When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following to show and measure ads, analyse performance and prevent fraud:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate its approximate location;
  • how you interact with the ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is sent to Google over encrypted connections and handled under Google's policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The app uses Google's consent system to request consent where required, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. From version 0.13.3, the age protections below apply before advertising starts. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, "Privacy choices" is available on the game's title screen to review or change choices, including applicable US state privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game or in Android's notification settings.

Children and teenagers

Arrow Dungeon is intended for players aged 13 and over. From version 0.13.3, the app asks for an age range before starting advertising. Ads are disabled until a range is selected and for players who select under 13. Advertising is restricted or disabled for younger players, with no personalised ads for under-18s and no personalised-ad consent request for under-16s. Where restricted ads are enabled, the app communicates the relevant age-treatment signals to Google. We do not receive your selected age range on a server of our own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

《箭之地牢》没有账号，也没有我们自己的服务器。你的游戏进度只保存在你的手机上。购买通过 Google Play 完成；广告由 Google AdMob 提供，它会按下文所述收集部分设备数据。

游戏在你手机上保存的内容

游戏会把你的进度、星星、金币、道具、设置和语言选择保存在本应用自己的存储中。从 0.13.3 版本起，应用还会在本地保存你选择的年龄范围，以应用广告保护措施；不会询问姓名或确切出生日期。我们不会接收这些游戏存档信息。卸载应用或清除存储会删除本地副本。如果启用且可用，安卓备份可能根据你的 Google 备份设置恢复部分应用数据。

购买

金币礼包、新手礼包和“移除广告”通过 Google Play 结算系统出售。付款及付款信息由 Google 处理，我们无法看到。应用在你的设备上使用购买凭证发放商品，并向 Google Play 确认购买。我们没有自己的购买服务器。Google 对购买的处理适用 Google 隐私权政策。

从 0.13.3 版本起，新购买的金币礼包只提供金币。新手礼包和“移除广告”可永久移除关卡之间的广告；使用同一 Google Play 账号可恢复此权益。奖励视频仍为自愿观看。更新会保留旧玩家现有存档中记录的免广告权益。已消耗的金币礼包无法通过 Google Play 恢复，因此这类旧权益依赖保留或恢复存档。如果遗失，请联系我们。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏会展示 Google AdMob 提供的广告：由你选择观看的奖励短视频（换取撤销、提示或复活），以及通过第一个区域后、在关卡之间偶尔出现的全屏广告。广告不会在关卡进行中出现。关卡间广告的移除权益见上文“购买”。启用广告时，为了展示广告、衡量效果、分析性能和防止欺诈，Google 移动广告 SDK 会收集：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）；
  • 你与广告和应用的互动，例如启动应用、点击和观看视频；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用启动时间和性能；
  • 设备标识符，例如安卓广告 ID 和应用集 ID。

这些数据通过加密连接发送给 Google，并按 Google 的政策处理。你可以阅读 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的应用的信息。应用使用 Google 的同意管理系统，在需要时征求同意，包括欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士。从 0.13.3 版本起，下述年龄保护措施会在广告启动前生效。当 Google 要求提供隐私选项入口时，游戏标题画面会显示“隐私选项”，供你查看或更改选择，包括适用的美国州隐私选项。你也可以在手机设置中重置或删除广告 ID。

提醒

如果你开启每日提醒，应用会在你的手机上安排通知，相关信息不会发送到任何地方。你可以在游戏中或安卓通知设置中关闭提醒。

儿童和青少年

《箭之地牢》面向13岁及以上玩家。从 0.13.3 版本起，应用会在启动广告前询问年龄范围。未选择年龄范围或选择未满13岁的玩家不会收到广告。对于较年轻的玩家，广告会受到限制或被停用：未满18岁不展示个性化广告，未满16岁不请求个性化广告同意。如果启用受限广告，应用会向 Google 传递相应的年龄保护信号。我们不会通过自己的服务器接收你选择的年龄范围。
